## THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up 23 November's world news: the AI rout that hit Asia (Kospi
−3.3%), the UN chief's call to regulate AI before it's too late, and the US lead
over China falling to a record low in the Asia Power Index. 31 stories in total:
3 hero scenes, 10 cards, and 18 more in the ALSO list, plus a world map and a
DATE CHECK box that keeps 22–21 Nov stories out of the day's news.

Built for the [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## How it works

- `draw.py` renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL).
- `main.py` (stdlib only) validates the PNG and copies it to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` — it never writes next to itself, so it also
  runs from a read-only copy.
